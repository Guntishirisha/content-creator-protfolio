About Me
The Girl Behind
the Content.

I'm Shirisha Gunti — a bilingual content creator, influencer, and writer from Hyderabad, India. I started my content journey in 2018 on TikTok before most people even knew what content creation was, built a following of 10,000+ there, and seamlessly transitioned to Instagram when TikTok was banned in India.

On Instagram, I've grown to 82,400+ organic followers through lipsync reels, lifestyle content, and fashion — creating content that genuinely resonates with my audience in both English and Telugu.

"Content creation isn't just what I do — it's been my identity for 7 years. I understand audiences, trends, and what makes people stop scrolling."

Beyond creating, I'm also a content writer — crafting captions, scripts, and brand copy that drives engagement. I've collaborated with 20+ brands across fashion, beauty, FMCG, entertainment, and technology, earning ₹1,00,000+ through authentic brand partnerships.
